The problem

Nobody notices until a customer or a regulator does.

A model can answer wrongly with total confidence. It can treat groups of people unevenly. It can change behaviour after a routine update. Often nobody notices until a customer or a regulator does.

Most teams have no single place to see what their AI systems do, why they do it, and what was done about it. Vesk is designed to be that place.

What we build

Four modules, one loop.

Check it, understand it, constrain it, record it.

Verify→Explain→Safeguard→Know↺

Verify

Check before you ship. Verify is intended to run tests on AI systems before and after release: accuracy, consistency, bias and failure cases. A change to a model or prompt gets tested instead of assumed safe.

Pre-launch

Explain

See why it said that. Explain is intended to show the inputs, context and steps behind an output. An engineer can debug it. A reviewer can follow it without reading the code.

Pre-launch

Safeguard

Set limits that hold. Safeguard is intended to apply rules while the system runs. Examples: stop personal data leaving the system, or flag an answer outside approved topics. Every time a rule triggers, it is logged.

Pre-launch

Know

Keep the record straight. Know is intended to keep an inventory of the AI systems in use: who owns each one, what data it touches, and what testing and decisions are on record. Questions from auditors or leadership can then be answered from one place.
