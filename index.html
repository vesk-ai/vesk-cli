A demo works. Production is where it counts.

A model can answer wrongly with total confidence. It can treat groups of people unevenly. It can change behaviour after a routine update.

Most teams have no single place to see what their AI systems do, why they do it, and what was done about it. Vesk CLI is that place.

Four modules · one record

01

Verify

Check before you ship.

Run tests on AI systems before and after release: accuracy, consistency, bias and failure cases. A change to a model or prompt gets tested instead of assumed safe.

✓Accuracy on held-out cases
✓Consistent across rephrasings
✕Uneven results by group
✓Refuses out-of-scope asks
02

Explain

See why it said that.

Show the inputs, context and steps behind an output. An engineer can debug it. A reviewer can follow it without reading the code.

inputcontextstepsoutput
03

Safeguard

Set limits that hold.

Apply rules while the system runs. Stop personal data leaving the system, or flag an answer outside approved topics. Every time a rule triggers, it is logged.

reply contains national ID number→ blocked · logged 14:02:11
04

Know

Keep the record straight.

Keep an inventory of the AI systems in use: who owns each one, what data it touches, and what testing and decisions are on record. Answer auditors and leadership from one place.

Credit scoringowner · risk team
Support assistantowner · platform
Résumé screeningowner · people ops
